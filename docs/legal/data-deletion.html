Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 17, 2026

You can request that your Deedew account and associated data be deleted at any time, including if you signed up or logged in using Google or Facebook.

How to request deletion

Email superdeedew@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete my account." Include your account email so it can be located.

What happens next

Your account and personal data (name, email, phone number, linked login provider) will be deleted within 30 days of the request. Records required for legal, security, or audit purposes (such as fraud-prevention logs) may be retained in de-identified form for a limited additional period, after which they are also removed.

Removing app access from Facebook or Google

You can also revoke Deedew's access to your account directly from the provider, at any time, independent of a deletion request to us:

  • Facebook: Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites → remove Deedew.
  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove Deedew's access.
