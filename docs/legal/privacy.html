Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Deedew is an independent, personal software project built for learning and portfolio purposes. This policy describes what data the app collects and how it's used.

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and phone number. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name and email address from that provider instead of asking you to create a password.

We also record basic technical information for security purposes: IP address, device/browser information, and login timestamps, so account activity can be audited and suspicious logins detected.

How we use it

Your data is used only to operate the app: authenticating you, showing you your account, and — for sellers — operating the marketplace features (listings, orders). We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

How we store it

Passwords are never stored in plain text — they're hashed with Argon2id before being saved. Login sessions use rotating tokens; only a hashed version of a session token is ever stored, so a database leak alone can't be used to log in as you.

Third-party sign-in

If you sign in with Google or Facebook, this app only requests your basic profile (name, email) — enough to create and recognize your account. We don't request access to your contacts, posts, or other data from those platforms.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, or request that it be deleted, at any time. See our data deletion instructions for how.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to superdeedew@gmail.com.
